Delete my Tessaris account

App: Tessaris (Android) · Publisher: Exuna · Last updated: 7 August 2026

This page explains how to delete your Tessaris account and the data attached to it. There are two routes: from inside the game, or by simple request if you no longer have the app.

The simplest way: from the game

Open Tessaris, then Profile → Danger zone → "Delete my account". The game asks you to confirm twice, once by retyping your nickname, then erases everything immediately.

If you no longer have the app

Write to studexu.contact@gmail.com with the subject Tessaris account deletion, stating the nickname you used online and, if you know it, your player tag (the characters shown under your nickname in the game). Those two details let us find the right account without asking you for anything else.

Requests are handled within 30 days, in practice much sooner. You get a confirmation by return email.

What is deleted

  • Your online profile: nickname, appearance, Elo rating, league and ranked record.
  • Your anonymous authentication account, that is, the technical identifier that linked you to that data.
  • Your Google Play Games cloud save, if you had one.
  • All your local data: progress, stars, coins, cosmetics, titles, chests, history and settings (when deleting from the game).

What is not deleted, and why

Games already played remain stored. A game belongs to your opponent as much as to you: erasing it at the request of only one of the two players would strip the other of their own history. These records contain the moves played, the scores and both participants' nicknames.

If you delete your account without going through the game, the data left on your phone is untouched: uninstall the app or clear its data from your Android settings.
