Privacy Policy

App: Tessaris (Android) · Publisher: Exuna · Last updated: 7 August 2026

This policy explains what data the Tessaris app processes, why, and how you stay in control.

In short

Tessaris is played alone, offline, without an account. Online multiplayer changes that: to match you with an opponent, the app creates an anonymous account and publishes the nickname you choose, your rating and your ranked record. Nothing else. No data is ever sold, and you can delete everything from inside the game.

1. Who is responsible

Exuna, an independent developer, publishes and operates Tessaris. Contact: studexu.contact@gmail.com

2. What stays on your device

Single-player works entirely offline. The following is stored locally and nowhere else: your campaign progress and stars, your coins, your cosmetic items, your titles, your chests, your match history, along with your settings (language, sound, vibration, visual quality) and your advertising choice. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data erases all of it.

3. Game account and online multiplayer

As soon as you open multiplayer, the app opens an anonymous account for you with Google Firebase Authentication. This account is tied to no email address, no phone number and no identity: it is a randomly generated technical identifier that lets the server recognise the same player from one game to the next.

The following is then stored on Google Cloud Firestore servers:

  • your public profile: the nickname you choose, your in-game appearance, your rating (Elo, league) and your ranked record. This profile is visible to other players — that is its whole purpose;
  • your matchmaking tickets, for the duration of a search;
  • the games you take part in: the moves played, the scores, the emotes exchanged and both players' nicknames;
  • the reports you submit, and any moderation decision concerning you.

Choose a nickname that does not identify you if you would rather not be recognised: it is public.

4. Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, you can copy your single-player progress to your Google account and get it back on another device. That save is stored by Google, on your own account, not by the publisher. It contains your progress, your coins and your cosmetics. You can delete it from the game, or at any time from the Google Play Games app, which lists your saved games.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. In doing so, Google processes device identifiers and technical data in order to select and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form is shown to you and you may refuse personalised advertising; ads still appear, but are not targeted. Google acts here under its own terms: policies.google.com.

6. Deleting your account and your data

In the game: Profile → Danger zone → "Delete my account". This erases your online profile, your anonymous account, your cloud save and all your local data. It is immediate and irreversible.

Without installing the game, or if you no longer have access to it: the procedure is described on the Delete my account page.

One exception, and it is deliberate: games already played are not deleted. A game belongs to your opponent as much as to you, and erasing it at the request of only one of the two players would strip the other of their own history.

7. What the publisher does not do

  • No selling, renting or trading of your data.
  • No access to your contacts, your location, your photos, your microphone or your camera. The app requests none of those permissions.
  • No advertising profile built by the publisher: advertising is handled entirely by Google.
  • No in-app purchases.

8. If you write to us

The feedback button in the game opens your email app with a pre-filled message. We then receive your address and the content of your message, used only to reply to you and to fix what you report.

9. Children

Tessaris is not intended for and not directed at children under 13. If you are the parent or guardian of a child under 13 who has created an online profile, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Processors and transfers

The app relies on services provided by Google: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Play Games and AdMob. These services may process data outside the European Union, under Google's contractual safeguards. There is no other recipient.

11. Retention

Your online profile is kept until you delete it. Matchmaking tickets disappear within minutes. Games played are kept for the history of the players who took part in them. Local data lives on your device until you erase it.

12. Your rights

Under applicable law, in particular the GDPR in the European Union, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection. Erasure is exercised directly in the game or through the deletion page; for anything else, write to studexu.contact@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

13. Changes

This policy may change along with the app. The date of the latest update appears at the top of this page. Significant changes will be announced in the game.
