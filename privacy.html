Privacy Policy

App: Tessaris (Android) · Publisher: Exuna · Last updated: July 16, 2026

This policy explains what data the Tessaris app processes, why, and how you stay in control. It is written to be clear rather than dense with jargon.

Summary

Tessaris has no account, no server, and does not sell any data. Your game data (nickname, progress, settings) stays on your device. The only data shared with third parties goes through the Google AdMob advertising service, and only according to your consent.

Who is responsible

The app is published by an independent developer (Exuna). For any question about this policy or your data, contact: studexu.contact@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

To work, Tessaris saves some information locally, on your phone only (via the app's storage). This information is neither sent to the publisher nor hosted on a server:

  • your chosen in-game nickname;
  • your progress: coins, levels, unlocked cosmetics, titles, settings;
  • your advertising consent choice.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes this information from your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Tessaris shows ads through Google AdMob. For this, the AdMob kit may process data such as device identifiers (including the advertising ID), technical device information, and ad-interaction data, in order to serve and measure ads.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form is shown before any ads. You can accept or decline personalized advertising.
  • If you decline, the app keeps working; non-personalized ads may be shown.
  • You can review your choice at any time from the "Privacy" section of the app's settings.

Google's processing is governed by its own rules. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data.

What the publisher does not do

  • No account, no sign-up, no login required.
  • No collection of contacts, precise location, photos, microphone or camera.
  • No sale of data to third parties.

If you contact us

If you use the "Feedback" button to send us a message, your email app opens and we then receive your email address and the content of your message. This is voluntary and used only to handle your feedback.

Children

Tessaris is not directed to children under 13, and the publisher does not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us for its deletion.

Publisher note: adjust to match the target audience declared on the Play Store ("Target audience and content") and the AdMob settings.

Your rights

Depending on your country (notably in the European Union, under the GDPR), you have rights of access, rectification and erasure. Since the publisher holds no data about you on a server:

  • your game data is deleted by uninstalling the app or clearing its data;
  • personalized advertising is managed via the app's "Privacy" section, or via your device settings (resetting the advertising ID);
  • for any other request, write to studexu.contact@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy may change if the app changes (for example, new features). The last updated date appears at the top of this page.

Contact

For any question: studexu.contact@gmail.com.
